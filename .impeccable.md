## Design Context

### Users
The product owner uses the September 23 research report to understand what people and agents publish, evaluate growth evidence, and decide what to build next.

### Brand Personality
Clear, thoughtful, evidence-led. Explain limits alongside conclusions without turning the page into a technical log.

### Aesthetic Direction
User-confirmed: plain white research report, near-black text, readable charts, concrete use cases, conclusions next to supporting evidence. Use a restrained scientific categorical palette with direct labels, sans-serif typography and generous section spacing. Each Cartesian chart names both axes and units; non-Cartesian diagrams explain their stages and visual encoding.

### Design Principles
- Lead with findings, then show the denominator and evidence.
- Separate measured facts, inferred use cases, and unknowns.
- Make month and source comparisons understandable on mobile.
- Publish aggregates and anonymized examples, not user content or identifiers.
- Use visual hierarchy and charts rather than a wall of identical cards.
